# Research notes: topics 6 to 22, run 3 September 2026

Seventeen documents were written by seventeen parallel research agents, each following `content/_meta/DEEP_RESEARCH_PROMPT.md` (plus the employer addendum for topics 11 to 15). Every cited URL was opened with WebFetch in the writing session on 2026-09-03. Every document passes `tools/corpus/verify.py` structurally (0 errors across 17 docs, 611 declared sources, 551 distinct URLs; 7 warnings, all lead-in lines like "As at September 2026:" that precede a cited list).

**`verify.py --net` has NOT been run.** Both the cloud sandbox and the Cowork VM sit behind a proxy that refuses CONNECT to public sites (403), so URL checking must be run on a normal machine: `python3 tools/corpus/verify.py --path content/inbox --net`.

**Shared limits hit during the run.** The session's WebSearch budget (200 calls) was shared by all seventeen agents and ran out early, and WebFetch went into a persistent 429 rate limit part-way through. Agents fell back to fetching known URLs. The effect is visible mostly in the employer directories: some big names could not be opened and are listed in each document's GAP blocks rather than in the tables. A second, search-enabled pass on the "missing employers" list below would close most of them.
