## In one line

As at September 2026, the big degree apprenticeship schemes mostly open between late September and early October for a start the following year, several fill on a rolling basis and close early when full, so the safest move is to apply in the first weeks after a scheme opens rather than waiting for a deadline.[^s13][^s17]

## Key facts

- Deloitte's early careers applications (including BrightStart apprenticeships) open on 21 September for Scotland, 28 September for Audit and Assurance, Legal and Tax Consulting, and 5 October for Advisory, Consulting, Enabling Functions and Real Estate; later programmes on the same page are dated January 2027 and March 2027.[^s17]
- Deloitte says some roles have a closing date but it closes applications early when it gets a very high volume, and recommends applying as early as possible.[^s17]
- PwC's undergraduate and entry level (school and college leaver) opportunities open on 30 September and graduate roles on 17 September; roles are filled on an ongoing basis and PwC may stop taking applications once volumes are high.[^s13]
- PwC's Flying Start degree programmes are applied for through UCAS, start in autumn 2027, and have a bursary application deadline of 12 March 2027.[^s11]
- BAE Systems' 2026 apprentice roles are closed and its 2027 applications open in January 2027, with early application advised.[^s20]
- Lloyds Banking Group recruits apprentices through the autumn each year, accepts applications on predicted grades, and requires the grades to be achieved before the start date.[^s24]
- EY's 2026 school leaver applications are closed and EY says it will reopen later in 2026 but gives no date.[^s18]
- KPMG gives apprentice job offers within 2 working days of its final "Launch Pad" stage; its last cycle held Launch Pad events from 25 November 2025 to 23 July 2026.[^s15]
- UCAS 2027 entry key dates: applications could be submitted from 1 September 2026; 15 October 2026 (18:00) for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, dentistry and veterinary; 13 January 2027 (18:00) for most courses; 30 June 2027 final standard deadline; Clearing opens 2 July 2027; 18 October 2027 last date to add a Clearing choice.[^s1]
- A level, AS, T Level and other level 3 results in England were issued on 13 August 2026; GCSE results on 20 August 2026.[^s2][^s3]
- AQA's provisional 2027 timetable gives Thursday 12 August 2027 as the day A level and level 3 results reach students, with schools receiving them on 11 August 2027; this is marked provisional.[^s4]
- You can apply for an apprenticeship while still at school, but you must be 16 or over by the end of the summer holidays to start it, and not in full-time education when you start.[^s7][^s8]
- National Apprenticeship Week ran 9 to 15 February 2026 and is listed as 8 to 14 February 2027.[^s9][^s10]
- UCAS says you can apply for as many apprenticeships as you like on top of your university application, and that apprenticeships are advertised at any time of year rather than in a single cycle.[^s1][^s5]

## When do the big degree apprenticeship employers open applications?

There is no single national apprenticeship deadline. UCAS says some employers advertise up to a year ahead and others only a month before the start date, and suggests starting to look around a year before you want to begin.[^s5] The dates below come from each employer's own site as at 3 September 2026. Where an employer publishes no date, that is stated, not guessed.

**Deloitte (BrightStart apprenticeships).** Applications open on 21 September for all Scotland-based roles; 28 September for Audit and Assurance, Legal and Tax Consulting; 28 September or 5 October for Actuarial and Technology depending on role; 5 October for Advisory, Consulting, Enabling Functions and Real Estate.[^s17] The page does not print a year next to those dates, but its later entries read "January 2027 (date TBC)" and "March 2027 (date TBC)", so the September and October dates fall in 2026.[^s17] Deloitte runs a strict one application per year policy.[^s17]

**PwC (school and college leaver programmes).** PwC's FAQ says undergraduate and entry level opportunities open on 30 September, with graduate roles opening on 17 September; no year is printed.[^s13] As at 3 September 2026 the entry level hub shows those programmes as closed for applications and Flying Start as open.[^s12] Flying Start degree programmes are applied for through UCAS, not PwC's own portal, start in autumn 2027, and close bursary applications on 12 March 2027.[^s11]

**KPMG.** KPMG's apprenticeship pages do not publish an opening or closing date.[^s14] The application process page lists nine in-person Launch Pad events for the most recent cycle, running from 25 November 2025 (London) to 23 July 2026 (London), which shows that KPMG's recruiting ran from autumn through to the following summer.[^s15]

**EY.** EY's schools page states that 2026 applications are closed and invites you to join its online community to be told when applications reopen "later this year"; no date is given.[^s18]

**BAE Systems.** 2026 apprentice roles are closed. BAE says it will reopen for 2027 opportunities in January 2027 and that early application is advised.[^s20]

**Lloyds Banking Group.** Its early careers site says programmes are currently closed and will open again in the autumn.[^s23] Its FAQ says it recruits through the autumn of each year and tells you to keep checking the website.[^s24]

**Bank of England.** Applications are closed and you can register interest for its 2027 opportunities; no dates are published.[^s26]

**Rolls-Royce.** As at 3 September 2026 the UK apprenticeships page lists no live vacancies and publishes no opening date; it describes a four-stage process of online application, online assessments, assessment centre and offer.[^s19]

**IBM, Accenture, Barclays and Network Rail.** None of these employers' apprenticeship landing pages publishes an opening date, closing date or start date; each points you to a live job search instead.[^s21][^s22][^s25][^s28]

**Civil Service.** Apprenticeships are advertised as individual vacancies on Civil Service Jobs as they arise, with an alert service; there is no single opening date. You must be 16 or over.[^s27]

**Dyson Institute.** The apply page asks for at least AAB at A level including A grades in Mathematics and one other science, technology or engineering subject, and describes four stages (application with project, online tests, phone interview, assessment day), but publishes no opening or closing date.[^s29]

**GTOD take:** the pattern across the employers above is that the professional services firms and banks open in late September or October and recruit through the winter, while some engineering employers (BAE) open in January. If a scheme you want is not open yet, register for its alerts now rather than checking back in spring.

## What does rolling recruitment mean, and why does applying early matter?

Rolling recruitment means the employer reviews applications and makes offers as they come in, rather than waiting for a closing date and then looking at everyone together. Once enough offers are accepted, the vacancy is closed even if a published closing date has not been reached.

PwC states that roles are filled on an ongoing basis unless the job description says otherwise, and that it may close to new applications after receiving a high volume, so it recommends applying early.[^s13] Deloitte says that although some roles show a closing date, it does close applications early when it gets a very high volume of submissions, and recommends applying as early as possible.[^s17] BAE Systems says early application is advised for its 2027 roles.[^s20]

**GTOD take:** treat the opening date, not the closing date, as your deadline. For a scheme that opens on 28 September, a strong application in the first two weeks is competing for every place; the same application in December may be competing for none. Prepare your CV, grades and answers before the scheme opens.

## How long does it take from applying to getting an offer?

Most employers do not publish an end-to-end timeline. Where they do state something:

- PwC says invitations to each stage usually give you seven days to complete it, and that hearing an outcome can take up to a month.[^s13] PwC allows only one application at a time; if unsuccessful you can reapply in the following intake year.[^s13]
- KPMG's apprentice process has four stages: explore and apply, online assessments, video interview, and Launch Pad, a half-day in-person event.[^s14][^s15] KPMG says successful candidates receive a job offer within 2 working days of Launch Pad.[^s15]
- Dyson Institute tells you after each of its four stages whether you have got through.[^s29]
- Rolls-Royce describes online application, online assessments, assessment centre and offer, with no timings.[^s19]

**GTOD take:** if you apply in October to a scheme with four stages, expect to be waiting on results through November and possibly into the new year. Do not stop applying elsewhere while you wait.

## When do apprenticeships start?

UCAS says apprenticeships are not tied to a single cycle and that start timing is agreed between you and the employer.[^s5] PwC's Flying Start programmes start in autumn 2027.[^s11] PwC does not offer deferred starts and tells you to apply for the intake year that matches when you want to begin.[^s13] Lloyds says any grades required by your offer must be achieved before your apprenticeship start date.[^s24]

**GTOD take:** the large school-leaver schemes covered here recruit through autumn and winter for a start the following late summer or September, matching the end of sixth form, but almost none of them prints a start date on their landing page. Check the individual vacancy, which normally states it.

> [!GAP]
> No employer page opened for this document (other than PwC Flying Start, "autumn 2027") stated an exact start date for 2027 school-leaver apprentices. The vacancy adverts themselves, once live, would settle this.

## How does this fit with A level and T level results day?

A level, AS and T Level results in England were issued on 13 August 2026, and GCSE results on 20 August 2026.[^s2][^s3] For 2027, AQA's provisional timetable has results reaching students on Thursday 12 August 2027, with schools receiving them the day before; AQA marks this provisional.[^s4]

Apprenticeship offers made during year 13 come before results, so they depend on you getting the grades. Lloyds accepts applications on predicted grades but says that if your offer requires specific qualifications you must achieve them before the start date.[^s24] Deloitte's BrightStart entry requirement is a minimum of 104 UCAS points from your top three A levels (or Highers in Scotland).[^s16]

**GTOD take:** an apprenticeship offer in year 13 is effectively conditional in the same way as a university offer. If results day goes badly, contact the employer that day, not the week after: some will accept near-miss grades, but they will not hold a place open indefinitely.

> [!GAP]
> The 2027 results day date has only been confirmed by AQA as provisional. Ofqual and JCQ pages opened for this document did not yet state the summer 2027 dates. Confirm on GOV.UK or jcq.org.uk before relying on 12 August 2027.

## How do apprenticeship deadlines line up with UCAS deadlines for 2027 entry?

UCAS 2027 entry dates: applications opened 12 May 2026; completed applications could be sent from 1 September 2026; 15 October 2026 at 18:00 for Oxford, Cambridge and most medicine, dentistry and veterinary courses; 13 January 2027 at 18:00 for most other undergraduate courses; Extra opens 25 February 2027; 30 June 2027 at 18:00 is the last date for standard applications; Clearing opens 2 July 2027; 23 September 2027 is the final application deadline; and 18 October 2027 is the last date to add a Clearing choice.[^s1]

The big apprenticeship schemes open in the same September and October window as the UCAS 15 October deadline, and are typically still open, or already closing early, around the 13 January UCAS deadline.[^s13][^s17] UCAS itself notes that the weeks before its October and January deadlines and the August results period are when students most actively look at apprenticeships.[^s6]

**GTOD take:** do both in parallel. Submit UCAS by 13 January 2027 as a safety net, and get your apprenticeship applications in as soon as each scheme opens in September and October 2026. The apprenticeship clock is faster than the UCAS clock.

## Can I apply for an apprenticeship in year 12?

The rules on age and school status come from GOV.UK. You can start an apprenticeship if you are 16 or over and not in full-time education, and you can apply while still at school as long as you will be 16 or over by the end of the summer holidays before you start.[^s7][^s8]

The two GOV.UK pages word the education rule differently: "Become an apprentice" says you must not be in full-time education, while "Apply for an apprenticeship" says not in education, full or part-time.[^s7][^s8] Both describe the position when you start, not when you apply, and "Become an apprentice" is the page that explicitly allows applying while still at school, so that is the one to rely on for timing.[^s8]

Nothing on GOV.UK stops a year 12 student applying. The practical barrier is timing: schemes opening in September and October 2026 are recruiting for people who will finish school in summer 2027, which is year 13.[^s13][^s17] PwC does not allow deferred starts and asks you to apply for the intake year matching your start date, so a year 12 applicant to a 2027 intake would have to leave school a year early to take it up.[^s13]

Predicted grades are accepted by Lloyds, with the condition that you achieve them before you start.[^s24] Deloitte states a minimum of 104 UCAS points from your top three A levels for BrightStart.[^s16] The Dyson Institute asks for at least AAB including Mathematics and one other science, technology or engineering subject.[^s29]

**GTOD take:** use year 12 to research, register for employer alerts, get work experience and attend insight events, then apply in the September and October of year 13. Apply in year 12 only for a scheme whose vacancy explicitly allows a start after you finish year 13, or where you plan to leave school after year 12.

> [!GAP]
> Most employer pages opened for this document do not state whether predicted grades are accepted (only Lloyds says so explicitly) or a minimum age beyond the GOV.UK rule (Civil Service states 16 or over). Check the specific vacancy advert.

## I have already applied to university through UCAS. Can I still apply for apprenticeships?

Yes. UCAS says you can apply for as many apprenticeships as you like on top of your university applications, that applying is more like applying for a job, and that positions can start at any time with deadlines that differ between employers.[^s1] Holding UCAS offers does not affect apprenticeship applications, which go directly to the employer, or through UCAS for a small number of employer-linked degrees such as PwC's Flying Start.[^s11]

**GTOD take:** if you accept an apprenticeship, you will need to decline your university place or withdraw from UCAS so you are not counted as holding two places for September. Do this once the apprenticeship contract is signed, not before.

> [!GAP]
> The UCAS page on withdrawing an application or declining a firm offer after accepting an apprenticeship was not opened for this document. Confirm the mechanics in the UCAS Hub before advising a student to withdraw.

## When is National Apprenticeship Week, and does it matter for applications?

National Apprenticeship Week 2026 ran from Monday 9 February to Sunday 15 February 2026.[^s9] Amazing Apprenticeships lists National Apprenticeship Week 2027 as 8 to 14 February 2027; the GOV.UK National Careers Service page opened for this document had not yet published the 2027 dates.[^s10][^s9] UCAS notes that awareness of apprenticeships among potential applicants is higher during the week than at other times of year.[^s6]

**GTOD take:** National Apprenticeship Week is useful for events and employer talks, but by mid-February the schemes that opened in September have been recruiting for four months and some will have closed early. Do not wait for the week to start applying.
