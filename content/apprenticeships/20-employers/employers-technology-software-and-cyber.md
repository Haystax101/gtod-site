## In one line

As at 3 September 2026, most big UK technology employers (Accenture, Amazon, BT, Capgemini, VodafoneThree, JLR, Kainos, BAE Systems) run their own Level 3 to Level 6 tech apprenticeships that closed for 2026 entry in spring and reopen for 2027 from January or February 2027, while Multiverse, QA and Firebrand are training providers that deliver the course, not the employer that hires you.[^s31][^s30][^s25][^s40]

## Key facts

- BT Group says it takes on more than 700 apprentices a year and, as at September 2026, lists starting pay for apprentices of £21,620 to £23,810 a year.[^s4][^s5]
- VodafoneThree's starting salary for its apprenticeships is £26,300 outside London and £28,940 in London, plus an annual bonus, and all its 2026 roles are marked closed as at September 2026.[^s15]
- JLR's Level 6 Digital and Technology Solutions degree apprenticeship pays £26,092, leads to a BSc with the University of Warwick (WMG), runs four years in the West Midlands, and its 2026 intake is closed with 2027 dates listed as TBC.[^s29][^s30]
- Amazon's Level 6 Software Development Engineer and Level 6 Cyber Security apprenticeships each last 42 months and start in September 2026; the cyber route asks for 112 UCAS points including one STEM Level 3 qualification.[^s8][^s9]
- Capgemini's Digital and Technology Solutions degree apprenticeship is with Sheffield Hallam University and asks for 3 A levels at grade C or above plus 7 GCSEs at grade 4 or above including English and maths.[^s12]
- Kainos' Earn as You Learn scheme is a four-year Computing Systems degree with Ulster University in Belfast, fees paid; 2026 recruitment is closed and the next campaign starts in February 2027.[^s25]
- BAE Systems says 2026 apprentice roles are closed and 2027 applications open in January 2027.[^s31]
- Virgin Media O2's careers page gives apprentice starting salaries between £24,000 and £30,000 depending on level, as at September 2026.[^s18]
- Multiverse, QA and Firebrand are training providers: the employer hires and pays you, the provider delivers the qualification.[^s40][^s41][^s42]

## What is the difference between an employer scheme and a provider programme?

An employer-run scheme is one the employer advertises, recruits for and pays. Amazon, BT, JLR, Kainos and VodafoneThree all recruit directly on their own careers sites.[^s6][^s4][^s30][^s25][^s15]

The employer still needs a training provider or university to deliver the qualification. Capgemini names Sheffield Hallam University for its degree route and Baltic Apprenticeships for its Level 3 IT Support route; JLR names the University of Warwick; Kainos names Ulster University.[^s12][^s29][^s25]

Multiverse, QA and Firebrand are providers, not employers. Multiverse says the hiring company recruits and employs the apprentice directly, and tells learners to search for "Multiverse" on the National Apprenticeship Service and then apply through the company's own link.[^s40]

QA describes itself as a training provider with 40 years of experience and lists apprenticeship categories including AI, Cloud, Cyber Security, Data, DevOps, IT and Software Development; individuals are sent to an apprenticeship platform to apply.[^s41]

Firebrand says at least 95% of its apprenticeships are spent learning at work, and its learner and employer links point to BPP's apprenticeship pages.[^s42]

**GTOD take:** If a job advert says "in partnership with Multiverse" or "delivered by QA", your employer is the company named in the advert, not the provider. Check who pays your wage before you apply.

## What does Accenture UK's apprenticeship involve?

Accenture's UK apprenticeship page lists three schemes: a Digital and Technology Solutions Professional apprenticeship leading to a BSc, a Level 6 AI Engineer apprenticeship, and a Chartered Manager apprenticeship at BSc level.[^s1]

The page says technology apprenticeships are available in six UK locations, the consulting apprenticeship is in London, and Accenture has hubs in Birmingham, Bristol, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Leeds, London, Manchester and Newcastle. Entry requirements, salary and application dates are not stated; the page sends applicants to an external Pinpoint recruitment portal.[^s1]

## What does IBM UK's apprenticeship involve?

IBM's UK careers page names an "IBM Apprenticeship Program" aimed at people without a degree, but gives no UK scheme names, levels, locations, entry requirements, pay or dates.[^s2]

The University of Exeter's page for its IBM partnership describes a four-year Level 6 Digital and Technology Solutions apprenticeship leading to a BSc, with five specialisms: Business Analysis, IT Consultancy, Cyber Security, Software Engineering and Data Analysis. Teaching is in week-long blocks at Exeter, and over 50 IBM degree apprentices have enrolled since the scheme started in 2016.[^s3]

## What does BT Group's apprenticeship involve?

BT's apprenticeship page lists Software Engineering, Engineering, Transformation and Delivery, Service, Data and AI, and EC Professions, and says BT helps more than 700 apprentices a year. Recruitment has four stages: online application, an untimed online assessment with five written questions and one video question, a final assessment with a competency interview and group exercise, then an outcome within two weeks.[^s4]

BT's FAQ page sets out four levels: Intermediate (Level 2), Advanced (Level 3 NVQ with a BTEC-type qualification), Higher (Level 4 with a foundation degree) and Full Degree. Programmes named include Research and Innovation, Cyber Security, Field Engineering and a Level 3 Unified Communications Technician. Length is 12 months to 4 years, all full time at 37.5 hours a week.[^s5]

As at 3 September 2026 the FAQ gives starting pay of £21,620 to £23,810 a year, rising against milestones. Most roles need 5 GCSEs at grade 4 to 9 including English and maths. The FAQ says vacancies open from Monday 2 February to Sunday 22 February but does not give the year.[^s5]

**GTOD take:** 2 February falls on a Monday in 2026, so that window almost certainly describes the 2026 intake. Expect a similar February window for 2027 but check jobs.bt.com in January. An older BT page still online refers to reviewing roles "for 2020", so ignore it.[^s49]

## What does Amazon's apprenticeship involve?

Amazon's UK landing page says applications open in February and programmes start between May and October, across teams including Information Technology, Software Development and Reliability Maintenance Engineering.[^s6] Its 2025 announcement said Amazon was creating more than 1,000 UK apprenticeships across more than 40 programmes, with around 2,000 apprentices already in the scheme, and placed third in the 2025 Top 100 Apprenticeship Employers.[^s11]

The Amazon Apprenticeships UK site lists technology programmes including IT Support, DevOps Engineer, Data Centre Operations IT, Cyber Security, Software Development Engineer, Software Tester and Data Analyst, plus Wales-based IT Engineer and Software Development Engineer routes.[^s7] Corporate offices are in Edinburgh, Manchester, Cambridge, Bristol and London.[^s10]

The Software Development Engineer Apprentice is a Level 6 BSc (Hons) Digital and Technology Solutions, software pathway, lasting 42 months from September 2026. You must be 18 or over before September 2026, have lived in the UK or EEA for three years, hold 5 GCSEs at grade 4 including maths and English, and have ABB at A level including maths or computer science, or BTEC Computing/IT at D*DD, or a T Level in Digital Production at Merit or above. Applications are rolling and close when enough are received.[^s8]

The Cyber Security Apprentice is a Level 6 BSc (Hons) Cyber Security Technical Professional in London, 42 months on a fixed-term contract at 40 hours a week, starting September 2026. It needs 5 GCSEs at grade 4 including maths and English and 112 UCAS points including one STEM Level 3 qualification. Salary is not stated.[^s9]

## What does Capgemini UK's apprenticeship involve?

Capgemini lists a Digital and Technology Solutions degree apprenticeship of about three years with Sheffield Hallam University including a 10-week in-person bootcamp, a four-year Digital UX degree apprenticeship with Manchester Metropolitan University, an Environmental Science degree apprenticeship with Kingston University, and a Level 3 IT Support Technician apprenticeship of about 18 months delivered by Baltic Apprenticeships with Cisco and Microsoft Azure certifications.[^s12]

The degree routes ask for 3 A levels at grade C or above and 7 GCSEs at grade 4 or above including English and maths. Roles are permanent and paid; salary and dates are not on the page.[^s12] A GOV.UK listing for Capgemini's 2026 Digital and Technology Solutions degree apprenticeship in Worthing closed on Friday 27 March 2026.[^s13]

## What does Cisco UK's apprenticeship involve?

Cisco's global apprenticeships page describes a pathway of work rotations, technical training and sometimes degree modules, and says each region and role has its own programme length and eligibility. It names no UK scheme, level, location, pay or date.[^s14]

## What does VodafoneThree's apprenticeship involve?

VodafoneThree lists Level 3 Field Engineering, IT Support and Pre-Sales; Level 4 Project Management, Product Management, Commercial and Network Engineering; Level 6 Network Engineering, MES Network Engineering, Data Analytics and AI Machine Learning; and an SCQF Level 8 Network Engineering route in Scotland. Locations include Farnborough, Newbury, Manchester, Birmingham, Exeter, Leiston and Norwich.[^s15]

Starting salary as at September 2026 is £26,300 outside London and £28,940 in London, plus an annual performance bonus. ## What does Sky's apprenticeship involve?

## What does Virgin Media O2's apprenticeship involve?

Virgin Media O2's careers page says schemes cover engineering, digital, business and customer roles at Levels 2 to 7, with starting salaries between £24,000 and £30,000 by level as at September 2026. You must be over 16 and some roles need a full UK driving licence.[^s18]

A company news release dated 12 December 2025 advertised 70 Field Technician roles (Level 2 Telecoms Field, 13 months, £26.5k, no qualifications needed, starting April or May 2026), 8 Network Engineer roles (£27k, start April 2026) and 4 Security and Fraud roles (from £24k, start April 2026), with deadlines between December 2025 and February 2026. Sage says its apprenticeships last 18 months to 4 years, are full time with about 20% off-the-job learning, and include mentoring and hybrid working three days a week in the office. ## What does Computacenter's apprenticeship involve?

Computacenter offers IT and business administration apprenticeships nationwide lasting one to five years at Levels 3, 4 and 6, with about 20% of time on training. Kainos' Earn as You Learn is a four-year scheme in Belfast: one day a week at Ulster University towards a Computing Systems degree, fees paid, on a full salary. ## What does Arm's apprenticeship involve?

Arm offers apprenticeships from Level 4 to Level 6 (a full degree) in Sheffield, Manchester and Cambridge, generally requiring three A levels or equivalent, with 20% of time on study. Pay and dates are in individual adverts.[^s26]

## What does Ocado's apprenticeship involve?

Ocado Group runs Higher and Degree apprenticeships after A levels leading to a BSc funded by Ocado, and Advanced apprenticeships after GCSEs in Cyber Security, HR Support or Business Administration. All are based at the Hatfield headquarters, with a four-stage process of application, phone interview, assessment day and offer.[^s27] The early careers page asks for at least 5 GCSEs and 2 A levels or equivalent.[^s28]

## What does JLR's digital apprenticeship involve?

JLR's Level 6 Digital and Technology Solutions degree apprenticeship is a four-year BSc with the University of Warwick (WMG), based in the West Midlands at Gaydon and Whitley, paying £26,092 as at September 2026. Pathways are Data Analytics or Software Engineering, taught in block weeks and online for three years with a work-based project in year four.[^s29]

Entry needs GCSE maths and English at grade 5 or above plus three more GCSEs at 5 or above, and A level maths at grade B (data) or C (software) plus one more A level from physics, chemistry, design and technology, ICT, computer science, economics or engineering at grade C. The 2026 intake is closed; JLR says applications open in February 2027 for a September 2027 start.[^s29][^s30]

## What does BAE Systems' apprenticeship involve?

BAE Systems' UK apprentices page says 2026 roles are closed and 2027 applications open in January 2027, and describes four levels: Intermediate (Level 2), Advanced (Level 3), Higher (Levels 4 and 5) and Degree (Level 6, taking 4 to 5 years). It claims 94% of apprentices stay on.[^s31] Neither page names Digital Intelligence cyber or software schemes; applicants are sent to the job search portal.[^s32]

## What does the Dyson Institute's apprenticeship involve?

The Dyson Institute offers a BEng (Hons) Engineering degree apprenticeship awarded by the Dyson Institute itself, at Dyson's Malmesbury campus, with two days a week of study and three days in the workplace in term time, a salary and no tuition fees.[^s33] Entry is at least AAB at A level including A grades in maths and one other science, technology or engineering subject.[^s34]

**GTOD take:** This is an engineering degree, not a software one, though Dyson's projects include software and electronics. Check the current programme page before applying, because one page on the site still refers to 2019 and 2020 arrangements.

## Does GCHQ run cyber apprenticeships?

GCHQ's "Working at GCHQ" page says some people start as apprentices, but gives no scheme names, levels, pay or dates.[^s35] The shared intelligence agencies vacancy portal had an "Apprentices and Interns" category but listed no apprentice vacancies on 3 September 2026.[^s36]

## What is CyberFirst and is it an apprenticeship?

CyberFirst is a government-backed outreach programme covering a girls' competition, free courses and a university bursary; it was run by NCSC and is moving to the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology.[^s37] The bursary is now delivered by DSIT under TechFirst as "Tech Grad".[^s38]

GOV.UK says the TechFirst Undergraduate Scholarship pays £4,000 a year of a degree with summer placements, existing CyberFirst bursary students move across from September 2026, and 2027/28 applications open in autumn 2026.[^s39]

**GTOD take:** CyberFirst is a bursary for university students, not an apprenticeship. It is not a route into GCHQ employment on its own.
