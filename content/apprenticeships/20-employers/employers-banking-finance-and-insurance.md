## In one line

As at 3 September 2026, at least 26 UK banks, investment firms, insurers and financial regulators publish apprenticeship pages, with published starting salaries running from the London Living Wage at the Financial Ombudsman Service to £29,500 for a London apprentice at Lloyds Banking Group.[^s44][^s5]

## Key facts

- Lloyds Banking Group pays apprentices £25,000 outside London and £29,500 in London, recruits every autumn, and asks for GCSE grade 4 to 9 (or National 5 A to C) in Maths and English, as at 3 September 2026.[^s5]
- Santander UK's Corporate and Commercial Banking apprenticeship pays £27,500, runs 33 months and leads to a Level 6 Financial Services Professional apprenticeship, as at 3 September 2026.[^s7]
- The FCA pays apprentices £25,700 in London and £23,500 in Leeds and Edinburgh; applications open February 2026 and the September 2026 cohort started on 7 September, as at 3 September 2026.[^s42]
- Aviva pays £26,700 on its Claims, Pricing and Underwriting, and Technology and Change pathways, all starting 7 September 2026, and asks for five GCSEs at 9 to 4 including English and Maths.[^s25][^s26][^s27]
- JP Morgan's Technology Degree Apprenticeship is a three year BSc (Hons) in Digital and Technology Solutions from the University of Exeter, in Bournemouth or London, needing BBB at A level with GCSE English 5 and Maths 6.[^s12]
- Goldman Sachs runs four year degree apprenticeships with Queen Mary University of London, the University of Warwick (Birmingham) and Walbrook Institute London; applications for the September 2027 intake open in autumn 2026.[^s14]
- UBS runs four year degree apprenticeships with the University of Exeter, needing ABB at A level, with a CFA Level 1 or Chartered Banker Institute qualification built into some streams.[^s18]
- BlackRock's 18 month apprenticeship is Level 4 Investment Operations Specialist in London and SCQF Level 6 in Edinburgh; the 2026 deadlines were 6 March (London) and 13 March (Edinburgh).[^s19]
- Aon's UK apprenticeship applications open each October, assessment centres run in February or March, and apprentices start at the end of September; you need five GCSEs at 9 to 4.[^s38]
- Legal & General apprenticeships run from Level 2 to 6 across Cardiff, Hove and London, typically ask for three Cs at A level (96 UCAS points), and open in February with rolling recruitment.[^s23]

## What does Barclays' apprenticeship involve?

Barclays splits its offer into two tiers. Standard apprenticeships are for people with GCSEs or National 5s and last two years. Higher and degree or graduate apprenticeships are for people with A levels or Highers and run up to four years, with qualifications up to degree level.[^s1]

Barclays says you are paid from day one and get a permanent role. The page lists many UK locations in its job alert menu, including London, Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds and Edinburgh. As at 3 September 2026 the page gives no salary, no named qualification or university, and no application dates.[^s1]

## What does HSBC's apprenticeship involve?

HSBC runs degree apprenticeships (around three years, degree-level study with no tuition fees to pay) and foundation or modern apprenticeships leading to GCSE or A level equivalent qualifications with a professional qualification.[^s2]

Technology apprenticeships sit in Sheffield at HSBC's tech centre. Most other programmes are in Birmingham, HSBC's UK head office. Other locations named are Leeds, Manchester, Hamilton, London and Chester. You must be 16 or over, a UK resident and not in full-time education.[^s2]

The degree apprenticeship page names two areas: technology (cyber, data, engineering, technical business analysis) and financial services. It promises a salary "reflective of your role from day one" and a fully funded qualification, but no figure, no university and no dates. HSBC says programme details appear in the apply section when applications open.[^s3]

## What does Lloyds Banking Group's apprenticeship involve?

Lloyds Banking Group lists 12 programmes: Accountancy Graduate Apprenticeship (Scotland), Accounting Professional and Finance Degree Apprenticeship (England), Actuarial, Audit, HR, Risk, Financial Services Professional, Digital Marketer, Digital Product Manager, Data, Technology Engineering Degree Apprenticeship (England) and Technology Engineering Graduate Apprenticeship (Scotland). The group says it has over a thousand apprentices and that new programmes are "coming 3rd November".[^s4]

The FAQ page is the useful one. Starting salary is £25,000 in the regions and £29,500 in London, as at 3 September 2026. Apprentices are permanent colleagues from day one with at least 22 days holiday rising to 30.[^s5]

Minimum entry is GCSE grade 4 to 9 in both Maths and English (England, Wales and Crown Dependencies) or National 5 at A to C (Scotland). Predicted grades count if you achieve them before the start date. Programmes run 12 months to three years. Recruitment happens each autumn. Locations include Lloyds, Halifax and Bank of Scotland branches UK-wide plus hubs in Belfast, Birmingham, Bristol, Cardiff, Chester, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Halifax, Leeds, London, Manchester and Newport.[^s5]

## What does NatWest Group's apprenticeship involve?

NatWest Group offers apprenticeships and degree apprenticeships in areas including digital and technology, customer service and project management, throughout the UK. Degree apprenticeships involve extra ability testing. Support comes from an Early Talent team, line managers and training provider coaches.[^s6]

As at 3 September 2026 the page gives no scheme names, levels, salary, entry requirements or dates. NatWest asks you to join its Early Talent community to be told when to apply.[^s6]

## What does Santander UK's apprenticeship involve?

Santander UK runs entry level, higher and degree level apprenticeships lasting from 12 months to four years. The named scheme is the Corporate and Commercial Banking Apprenticeship Programme: 33 months, three 12 month placements and a final nine month placement, leading to a Level 6 Financial Services Professional apprenticeship (degree equivalent). It pays £27,500 as at 3 September 2026 and asks for 112 UCAS points from your top three A levels, excluding General Studies and EPQ.[^s7]

The main hub is a new head office in Milton Keynes, with London and business centres across the UK (North, London and South East, South West and Wales, Midlands, Scotland).[^s7] Each scheme sets its own entry requirements. Selection is an online interview and three short assessment games, then an assessment day. Apprenticeship roles cannot be visa sponsored. Roles are permanent on condition you complete the qualification.[^s8]
