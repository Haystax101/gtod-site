## In one line

As at 3 September 2026, all four Big Four firms and at least a dozen mid-tier UK accountancy firms run school leaver apprenticeships that lead to a professional qualification (usually ACA via ICAEW, or ATT/CTA for tax), but since 1 January 2026 government funding for a new Level 7 start in England only covers people aged 16 to 21 when they begin.[^s1][^s16][^s19]

## Key facts

- From 1 January 2026, Level 7 apprenticeships in England are government-funded only for people aged 16 to 21 at the start, or under 25 if they are a care leaver or have an Education, Health and Care plan; anyone who started before that date is funded to completion.[^s1]
- The Level 7 "Accountancy or taxation professional" standard is the one Big Four and mid-tier firms use for ACA, ACCA, CIMA and CTA training; it typically takes 36 months and its funding band is a maximum of £21,000.[^s2]
- ICAEW's Level 7 Accountancy Professional apprenticeship leads to the ACA chartered accountant qualification; its Level 4 Accounting Technician apprenticeship leads to ICAEW CFAB.[^s3]
- ACCA's Level 4 Professional Accounting Technician apprenticeship gives the ACCA Diploma in Accounting and Business, and its Level 7 apprenticeship leads to eligibility for ACCA membership; ACCA states Level 7 funding is only for people under 22 when they start, from 1 January 2026.[^s6]
- CIMA's Level 4 route gives the Certificate in Business Accounting and its Level 7 route gives the full CIMA professional qualification.[^s7]
- AAT's Level 4 Professional Accounting Technician apprenticeship includes the AAT Level 4 Diploma in Professional Accounting, takes 18 to 24 months and has a funding cap of £12,000.[^s10]
- KPMG is the only firm in this directory that publishes a salary: its five-year Audit Apprenticeship pays £25,500 to £28,000 depending on programme and location, as at 3 September 2026.[^s16]
- Published UCAS point asks as at 3 September 2026: Deloitte 104, PwC 112, Crowe 112, S&W 112, KPMG 120, Saffery 120; Grant Thornton and EY say they have no minimum grade requirement.[^s14][^s12][^s25][^s27][^s16][^s26][^s20][^s17]
- PwC's entry level programmes and EY's 2026 apprenticeships were closed to applications as at 3 September 2026; Grant Thornton was open for its 2026 apprentice programmes.[^s11][^s17][^s19]
- No employer page opened for this directory mentions the January 2026 Level 7 age rule; only ACCA's page and the DfE and Skills England pages do.[^s6][^s1][^s2]

## What is the Level 7 funding change and does it affect me?

From 1 January 2026, the government only funds a new Level 7 apprenticeship in England if the apprentice is aged 16 to 21 at the start.[^s1] Care leavers and people with an Education, Health and Care plan keep funding up to age 24 at the start.[^s1] Anyone who started a Level 7 apprenticeship before 1 January 2026 stays funded to the end.[^s1]

The government's stated reason is to move money towards young people at the start of their working lives rather than people already in work with higher qualifications.[^s1] The decision was announced on 27 May 2025 and took effect on 1 January 2026.[^s5] ICAEW's chief executive publicly responded to the decision to remove funding for people over 21.[^s4]

For accountancy, the standard affected is "Accountancy or taxation professional" at Level 7, which typically takes 36 months and has a maximum funding band of £21,000.[^s2] Skills England's page for that standard now carries the 16 to 21 rule.[^s2] ACCA's page puts it as: from 1 January 2026, Level 7 funding is only available to students under 22 when they start.[^s6]

If you are 16 to 19 and applying now, you are inside the funded age group, so the change should not stop an employer training you to ACA, ACCA, CIMA or CTA at Level 7.[^s1] The people it hits hardest are career changers and graduates aged 22 or over.[^s1]

**GTOD take:** the rule is about who the government pays for, not who is allowed to do the qualification. An employer can still pay for an over-21 to study ACA outside the apprenticeship system, or start them on a Level 4 apprenticeship (which stays funded at all ages) and fund the rest itself. Ask any employer what they do for over-21s before you assume you are excluded.

## Which employer pages have not been updated for the Level 7 change?

As at 3 September 2026, none of the employer pages opened for this directory mention the January 2026 age rule, even where the page names a Level 7 qualification.[^s16][^s17][^s19][^s27][^s35] KPMG (five-year ACA/CA programme), EY (Level 7 Accountancy and Taxation), Grant Thornton (Level 4 then Level 7), S&W (most programmes Level 7) and Bishop Fleming (Level 7 ACA) all describe Level 7 training without an age note.[^s16][^s17][^s19][^s27][^s35]

PwC's entry level page states there is no age restriction for its programmes; the schemes it lists are Level 4 or ATT-based rather than Level 7, so that statement may still be accurate for those schemes.[^s12]

Among professional bodies, ACCA's England apprenticeships page has been updated.[^s6] The ICAEW student leaflet available online is a 2018 document that says the route is open at any age, which predates the change; ICAEW's newer employer blog and news release do cover it.[^s3][^s5][^s4] The two CIMA pages opened say nothing about the change.[^s7][^s8]

**GTOD take:** treat an employer page's silence as "not updated yet", not as evidence that the employer will fund over-21s. If you are 22 or over, ask the recruiter directly.

## What are ACA, ACCA, CIMA, ATT/CTA and AAT?

ACA is the chartered accountant qualification from ICAEW. The Level 7 Accountancy Professional apprenticeship leads to ACA and typically takes 36 to 48 months depending on prior experience; the 2018 ICAEW leaflet lists entry as two A level passes (A* to E) plus three GCSEs at 9 to 4 or A to C.[^s3] ICAEW's Level 4 Accounting Technician apprenticeship leads to ICAEW CFAB (Certificate in Finance, Accounting and Business) and typically takes 18 to 24 months.[^s3]

ACCA is a global accountancy body. Its Level 4 Professional Accounting Technician apprenticeship gives the ACCA Diploma in Accounting and Business, rated as equivalent to the first year of a degree, and most people finish it in about a year; it is funded at all ages.[^s6] Its Level 7 Accountancy Professional apprenticeship typically takes 36 months, ends with a project report and the Strategic Business Leader exam, and leads to eligibility for ACCA membership.[^s6]

CIMA is the management accountancy body. Its Level 4 route gives the CIMA Certificate in Business Accounting through the Professional Accounting/Tax Technician standard, and its Level 7 route gives the full professional qualification through the Level 7 Professional Accountant standard.[^s7] CIMA members can hold the CGMA designation.[^s8] CIMA's page says apprentices must be over 16, not in full-time education and living in England.[^s7]

ATT and CTA are tax qualifications. ATT is the tax technician level and CTA is the chartered tax adviser level; PwC's Tax programme works towards ATT with the option of CTA afterwards, and Saffery offers ATT then CTA, or ACA then CTA.[^s12][^s26] Both sit under the same Level 7 standard as ACA, which lists the Chartered Institute of Taxation as one of the membership routes.[^s2]

AAT is the accounting technician body. AAT supported three apprenticeship standards at Levels 2, 3 and 4.[^s9] The Level 4 Professional Accounting Technician apprenticeship includes the AAT Level 4 Diploma in Professional Accounting, takes 18 to 24 months, has a funding cap of £12,000, and completing the full diploma makes you eligible for AAT membership (MAAT).[^s10] KPMG uses AAT Level 3/4 as the first stage before ACA.[^s16]

## Which firms count as "Big Four" and "mid-tier"?

The Big Four are PwC, Deloitte, KPMG and EY.[^s12][^s14][^s16][^s17] Mid-tier firms covered here are Grant Thornton, BDO, RSM, Crowe, Saffery, S&W, Azets, MHA, HaysMac, PKF Littlejohn, Johnston Carmichael and Bishop Fleming.[^s19][^s22][^s24][^s25][^s26][^s27][^s30][^s31][^s32][^s33][^s34][^s35]

Accenture was checked because it is consulting-adjacent, but its UK apprenticeship page lists only technology and consulting degree apprenticeships (Digital and Technology Solutions Professional, a Level 6 AI Engineer route, and Chartered Manager), with no accounting or finance qualification, so it belongs in a technology directory.[^s37]

### What does PwC's apprenticeship involve?

PwC's entry level page describes programmes aimed at school and college leavers or career changers, and says they are for people without a degree.[^s11] As at 3 September 2026, the entry level programmes were closed to applications while the Flying Start degree programme was open.[^s11]

The entry level programmes page lists three routes.[^s12] Consulting is a two-year programme with a Level 4 Junior Management Consultant apprenticeship from CIMA.[^s12] Operate (audit, accounting, tax and finance work) includes an Accounting Technician qualification from Accounting Technicians Ireland.[^s12] Tax works towards ATT, with the option of CTA afterwards.[^s12]

Entry requirements are 112 UCAS points from up to three A levels or equivalent, plus GCSE grade 4 or above in English Language and Maths.[^s12] PwC states there is no age restriction.[^s12] Locations are Belfast, Birmingham, Bradford, Bristol, East Midlands, Leeds, London and Manchester, with a September 2027 start and at least three days a week in the office or at a client.[^s12] Salary is not stated.[^s12]

Flying Start is different: it is a full accounting degree at Newcastle, Manchester, Reading or Queen Mary University of London, applied for through UCAS, with paid work placements at PwC each year and 85% of ICAEW exams done by graduation.[^s13] There is a bursary of up to £10,000 over four years for households earning under £35,000, with a bursary deadline of 12 March 2027 for the autumn 2027 start.[^s13]

### What does Deloitte's apprenticeship involve?

Deloitte's BrightStart Higher Apprenticeship runs in Actuarial, Advisory, Audit & Assurance, Consulting, Enabling Functions, Deloitte Legal, Real Estate, Tax Consulting and Technology.[^s14] It takes between 15 months and four years depending on the area and ends in a permanent job.[^s14]

You need a minimum of 104 UCAS points from your top three A levels, must have finished A levels or Highers, and must not already have a degree.[^s14] Deloitte has offices across the UK and offshore, and says it closes applications early when it gets a high volume.[^s14] Salary and the exact apprenticeship level are not stated on the page.[^s14]

Deloitte's 1 September 2026 press release says 182 BrightStart apprentices joined this year across 21 UK offices, and quotes an Audit & Assurance apprentice studying for the ACA.[^s15] Deloitte also runs Level 3 entry apprenticeships (Business Administration or CIPD Human Resources) at its Cardiff delivery centre only.[^s14]

### What does KPMG's apprenticeship involve?

KPMG's Audit Apprenticeship is a five-year programme covering AAT Level 3/4 and then the ACA, or the ICAS CA in Scottish offices.[^s16] Training is blended, mixing live online classes, recorded courses and classroom sessions, with dedicated study leave.[^s16]

Entry requirements are five GCSEs at 4 to 9 (A* to C) including English Language and Maths at grade 5 or B, plus 120 UCAS points from your top three A levels or Scottish equivalents; KPMG encourages people slightly below these to apply with contextual information.[^s16] Salary is £25,500 to £28,000 depending on programme and location, as at 3 September 2026.[^s16] Vacancies are listed by city (for example London and Edinburgh for autumn 2026), and KPMG invites you to sign up to be told when applications open.[^s16] Anyone with an accountancy degree is asked to contact recruitment before applying.[^s16]

### What does EY's apprenticeship involve?

EY's schools page lists Audit, Tax and Finance Operations business apprenticeships, plus Digital & Technology and Business Leadership and Management Practice degree apprenticeships, and Career Starters and Smart Futures work experience.[^s17] EY says it removed the requirement for minimum UCAS points or a degree classification, and its apprenticeship provision was rated Outstanding by Ofsted in 2024.[^s17] The 2026 applications were closed as at 3 September 2026.[^s17]

The student recruitment site describes the Audit Apprenticeship Programme as four years, studying with ICAEW in England, ICAS in Scotland and Chartered Accountants Ireland in Northern Ireland, on the Level 7 Accountancy and Taxation apprenticeship in England.[^s18] Salary is described as competitive without a figure.[^s18] Northern Ireland recruits must stay in Northern Ireland for the whole programme.[^s18]

### What does Grant Thornton's apprenticeship involve?

Grant Thornton's apprenticeship page describes four or five years of work and study in Audit, Tax or Advisory, studying towards two qualifications: a Level 4 and then a Level 7 apprenticeship, fully funded by the firm.[^s19] It does not name ACA, ACCA or CTA on the page.[^s19] The firm says it has a flexible approach and does not ask for set grades or subjects, and was open for 2026 apprentice applications as at 3 September 2026.[^s19]

The FAQs say there are no minimum requirements beyond essentials such as right to work, that the firm pays all qualification and exam costs, and that trainees can take a £2,500 interest-free loan at the start.[^s20] You can only apply once a year; if unsuccessful you reapply the following August or September.[^s20] There are 19 UK locations, including London, Manchester, Birmingham and Edinburgh.[^s20] Grant Thornton also runs Talent Track, a four-year Accounting and Finance programme with the University of Leeds.[^s21]

### What does BDO's apprenticeship involve?

BDO's school leaver page describes a four-year programme in which you earn a salary, gain experience and work towards a professional qualification.[^s22] Areas mentioned are Audit, Advisory, Tax and the Shared Service Centre; the qualification depends on the business area and is not named on the page.[^s22] BDO offers paid study leave and says many school leavers are fully qualified by their mid-twenties.[^s22]

The main BDO careers site says apprentices can become a qualified accountant or tax professional within four years and that BDO funds all training.[^s23] Entry requirements, locations, salary and dates are not stated on either page.[^s22][^s23]

### What does RSM's apprenticeship involve?

RSM's school leaver page lists four routes: Audit, Tax, Consulting and Digital.[^s24] It says you work towards a chartered accountancy qualification alongside your cohort and RSM's graduates, with mentors and team support, and that most school leavers do not know exactly what they want when they apply.[^s24] Level, qualification body, salary, locations, duration and dates are not stated; the page points to RSM's job portal.[^s24]

### What does Crowe's apprenticeship involve?

Crowe has over 440 trainees across Audit, Tax, Advisory and Consulting.[^s25] Most trainees study for the ACA with ICAEW, while some follow tax or business solutions routes.[^s25] School leavers spread their studies over four to five years, against three for graduates.[^s25]

Entry requirements are 112 UCAS points from three A levels (or a 2:2 degree for graduates) plus GCSE grade 4 (C) or above in Maths and English Language.[^s25] Salary, locations and dates are not stated.[^s25]

### What does Saffery's apprenticeship involve?

Saffery's school leaver programmes combine a full-time salary with client work and study.[^s26] Accounting trainees take ACA, ICAS CA or ACCA over 3.5 to 4.5 years; Tax trainees take ATT and CTA, or ACA and CTA, over the same period; a small number of Payroll roles lead to CIPP Level 3 and 5.[^s26]

You need at least 120 UCAS points from your top three A levels (excluding General Studies and AS levels) and at least a B or 6 in GCSE Maths and English Language.[^s26] The firm offers a 35-hour week with core hours 10am to 4pm, 25 days' holiday and a profit-sharing plan.[^s26] Salary and locations are not stated.[^s26]

### What does S&W's apprenticeship involve?

S&W is the accountancy and tax business that used to be Evelyn Partners' professional services arm.[^s28] Its early careers page says many programmes are Level 7 apprenticeships, and the school leaver page says most are.[^s28][^s27] Routes are Audit (Assurance and Business Services), Tax (Business Tax or Private Client Tax) and Accountancy.[^s27]

Entry requirements are a predicted or achieved 112 UCAS points from your top three A levels excluding General Studies, plus GCSE grade 5 in English Language and Maths.[^s27] S&W funds the qualification, gives study leave and pairs you with a buddy.[^s27] Salary, locations, duration and dates are not stated; roles are on its SmartRecruiters portal.[^s27]

### What does Azets' apprenticeship involve?

Azets' early careers page says it is an AAT accredited employer, an ICAEW approved training employer and an ACCA platinum accredited firm, and also trains for ICAS.[^s30] Areas are Audit, Tax and Business Advisory.[^s30] Scheme names, levels, locations, entry requirements, salary and dates are not on the page; it links to an "Apply now" portal.[^s30]

### What does MHA's apprenticeship involve?

MHA's Accountancy and Tax Apprenticeship is for school leavers who want an alternative to university, with two to four years of on-the-job training and qualification in five years or less.[^s31] MHA stresses determination rather than specific grades, offers hybrid and flexible working, and says many of its partners started as apprentices.[^s31] Locations, salary and deadlines are not stated.[^s31]

### What does HaysMac's apprenticeship involve?

HaysMac (formerly Haysmacintyre) runs an Audit and Tax Apprenticeship Scheme lasting 4.5 years at its central London office, with a competitive but unstated salary.[^s32] The careers page links to a register-your-interest form for its January 2027 early careers intake.[^s32] Level and qualification body are not stated.[^s32]

### What does PKF Littlejohn's apprenticeship involve?

PKF Littlejohn's early careers page describes a school leaver apprenticeship programme offering a professional qualification, a competitive salary and early responsibility, across its London, Leeds and Manchester offices.[^s33] Level, qualification, entry requirements, salary figure and dates are not stated.[^s33]

### What does Johnston Carmichael's apprenticeship involve?

Johnston Carmichael's JC Futures Programme is a four to six year route for school or college leavers to qualify as accountants, based across 13 locations in Scotland and beyond.[^s34] Qualifications offered are ICAS, ACCA and ATT/CTA, with graduate apprenticeship options at Robert Gordon University or Glasgow Caledonian University.[^s34] The firm pays tuition and gives study leave, and the first year focuses on settling in from school.[^s34] Applications were closed as at 3 September 2026, with salary described as competitive.[^s34]

### What does Bishop Fleming's apprenticeship involve?

Bishop Fleming's Launchpad programme is a five-year school leaver route to a Level 7 apprenticeship with the ACA, delivered with training provider First Intuition, rotating through accounts, audit and tax.[^s35] Offices are Bath, Birmingham, Bristol, Cheltenham, Exeter, Plymouth, Torquay, Truro and Worcester.[^s35] The firm says it is not looking for maths specialists and does not list grade requirements; recruitment usually starts in mid-November on a rolling basis with most starts in September.[^s35]

The careers homepage shows a live School Leaver Trainee Accountant vacancy (ACA and ACA/CTA) for September 2027, and says the firm ranked 17th in the Sunday Times Top 100 Apprenticeship Employers 2026 with an Outstanding Ofsted rating.[^s36] Salary is not stated.[^s35][^s36]
