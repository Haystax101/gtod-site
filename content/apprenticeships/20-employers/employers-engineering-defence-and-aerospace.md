## In one line

As at 3 September 2026, most big UK engineering, defence and aerospace employers have closed their 2026 apprenticeship intakes and will reopen for 2027 between autumn 2026 and early 2027, with published starting pay ranging from about £17,400 at Level 3 to about £26,000 at degree level.[^s1][^s13][^s8]

## Key facts

- BAE Systems' 2026 apprentice roles are closed and it says it will reopen for 2027 in January; it runs Level 2, 3, 4/5 and 6 schemes and says 94% of its apprentices stay on.[^s1]
- Rolls-Royce publishes its apprentice pay: as at April 2026, year one is £18,316.40 on the Advanced (Level 3) scheme, £21,512.27 on the Higher scheme and £23,932.59 on the Degree scheme.[^s2]
- JLR pays £26,092 on its Level 6 and Level 7 programmes and about £17,393 on its Level 3 Advanced Apprenticeship; all its apprenticeships last four years and degree routes are with the University of Warwick.[^s13][^s14]
- Sellafield Ltd's degree apprentices earn £23,917 in year one rising to £31,795 in year four, plus a bonus of up to £4,000; entry is 5 GCSEs at grade 4 plus 96 UCAS points.[^s26]
- Bentley pays £22,662 to Level 3 and Level 4 apprentices and £24,929 to Level 6 apprentices; recruitment ran February to April 2026 with a start on 7 September 2026.[^s19]
- MBDA's apprentice salaries start at £18,000 with a £3,000 joining bonus; degree apprenticeships typically need 5 GCSEs and 120 UCAS points.[^s8]
- The RAF pays £25,200 a year from day one to apprentices, with roles from Level 2 to Level 7 and ages mostly 16 to 47.[^s35]
- Airbus UK applications run from October until February, need five GCSEs at grade 4 including maths, English and a science, and the roles cannot sponsor visas.[^s3]
- Defence employers require security vetting: BAE Systems says higher vetting levels need 5 to 10 years of continuous UK residency, and Leonardo says SC clearance normally needs 5 years' permanent UK residency.[^s1][^s5]

## What does BAE Systems' apprenticeship involve?

BAE Systems describes four tiers: Intermediate (Level 2) for entry-level specialist trades such as joinery or painting, Advanced (Level 3) in engineering, technology and business, Higher (Level 4 and 5) as an alternative to university, and Degree (Level 6) taking four to five years.[^s1]

As at 3 September 2026 the page says 2026 apprentice roles are closed and 2027 roles will open in January.[^s1] It claims 94% of apprentices stay on with the company and cites a number one ranking in the Department for Education's Top 100 Apprenticeship Employers 2026.[^s1]

Security matters here. Every applicant must pass the Baseline Personnel Security Standard, and roles needing higher vetting require 5 to 10 years of continuous UK residency.[^s1] No salary figures, universities or specific site lists appear on the page.[^s1]

## What does Rolls-Royce's apprenticeship involve?

Rolls-Royce lists Young Apprenticeships, Advanced/Modern Apprenticeships (Level 3), Higher Apprenticeships and Degree Apprenticeships across aerospace engineering, submarines engineering and business.[^s2] Level 3 entry is post-GCSE; Higher and Degree entry is post-A level or equivalent, and the page says there is no upper age limit.[^s2]

Level 3 roles are listed at Derby, Annesley, Bristol, Rotherham, Washington, Inchinnan, Ansty, Solihull, Thurso, East Grinstead and Barnoldswick.[^s2]

Pay is unusually transparent. As at April 2026: Advanced runs £18,316.40 in year one to £27,316.88 in year four; Higher £21,512.27 to £32,082.58; Degree £23,932.59 in year one to £34,831.86 in year four, with a year five finance figure of £39,795.40.[^s2] No application dates or universities are named.[^s2]

## What does Airbus UK's apprenticeship involve?

Airbus UK runs Craft, Technician and Degree apprenticeships from Level 2 up to Levels 6 and 7 (bachelor's or master's), in engineering, manufacturing, IT and digital, procurement, cyber security, facilities and business administration.[^s3] Sites are Belfast, Broughton, Chippenham, Filton, Newcastle upon Tyne, Newport, Portsmouth, Stevenage, Oxford and Shawbury.[^s3]

Typical entry is five GCSEs at grade 4 or C including maths, English language and a science; degree routes also need A levels or a BTEC.[^s3] Applications open in October and close in February.[^s3]

You must show right to work in the UK for the whole programme, and Airbus says its apprenticeships do not meet the UK Visas and Immigration minimum for sponsoring migrant workers.[^s3] No salary is published.[^s3]

## What does Leonardo UK's apprenticeship involve?

Leonardo's current careers page says its apprenticeships cover engineering, business and cyber security, last two to five years, and that over 400 graduates and apprentices are in training with over 90% completing.[^s4] As at 3 September 2026 all 2026 programmes are closed, and 2027 programmes open in autumn 2026.[^s4]

An older Leonardo page lists the scheme detail: Manufacturing (3 years, BTEC Level 2 and 3), Technical (4 years), Business (2 years, Level 3), Cyber Security (4 years, Level 4 Cyber Security Technologist), Design Engineer Technical (HND) at the electronics sites in Basildon, Bristol, Edinburgh, Luton, Southampton and Filton, plus Craft (4 years) and Electrical or Mechanical Engineering Degree apprenticeships (5 years) at the Yeovil helicopters site.[^s5] Scottish routes include a Software Engineering Graduate Apprenticeship leading to a BSc (Hons) in Software Development for Business.[^s5]

Entry examples from that page: Technical needs 5 GCSEs at 4-9 including English, maths and science; Manufacturing needs 2 GCSEs at 4-9 including English and maths.[^s5] Applicants must be eligible for SC security clearance, which normally means at least 5 years' permanent UK residency.[^s5]

**GTOD take:** the roles page still shows 2023 application dates, so treat its scheme list as indicative and check the live careers site before relying on it.

## What does Babcock's apprenticeship involve?

Babcock offers Intermediate, Advanced, Higher and Modern (Scotland) apprenticeships, Degree and Graduate apprenticeships, and Engineering Trades apprenticeships in mechanical fitting, electrical fitting, CNC machining, fabrication, pipefitting and welding.[^s6] Subject areas are business, engineering and science, project management and skilled trades.[^s6]

Locations include Bristol, Plymouth (Devonport), HMNB Clyde at Faslane, Rosyth, Warrington, Leicester, Portsmouth and other defence sites.[^s6] Degree and Graduate apprenticeships open in October; Intermediate, Advanced, Higher and Modern apprenticeships open in December.[^s6] The page gives no level numbers, salaries, universities or grade requirements, and points to a separate nationality and security section.[^s6]

## What does QinetiQ's apprenticeship involve?

QinetiQ's apprenticeship page lists roles in science, technology and business, giving Aeronautical Avionics Maintenance Engineering, Mechanical Manufacturing Engineering, business administration, project management and facilities management as examples.[^s7] It says QinetiQ works from over 35 UK locations and runs an Apprentice Training School at MOD Boscombe Down near Salisbury.[^s7]

The UK early-careers page adds only that apprenticeships are open to school leavers and career changers, pointing to a vacancy search.[^s38] Neither page states levels, entry requirements, pay or dates, and the apprenticeship page still refers to a 2021 recruitment window.[^s7]

## What does MBDA UK's apprenticeship involve?

MBDA lists: General Engineering (Level 4 Engineering Manufacturing Technician), Mechanical Engineering degree (BEng (Hons)), Electrical Engineering degree (BEng (Hons) Electrical and Electronic Engineering), Software degree (BSc (Hons) Digital and Technology Solutions), Business Management degree (BA (Hons)), Project Controls (Advanced), Finance (Level 3 Assistant Accountant progressing to Level 4) and Manufacturing Operator (Level 3 Engineering Fitter).[^s8] Sites are Stevenage, Bolton and Bristol.[^s8]

Entry: Advanced typically 5 GCSEs; Higher 5 GCSEs plus 96 UCAS points; Degree 5 GCSEs plus 120 UCAS points.[^s8] Pay starts at £18,000 with a £3,000 joining bonus in the first month's salary, on a four-year scheme.[^s8] As at 3 September 2026 the September 2026 intake is closed and you can register interest for 2027.[^s8] The page does not name universities or state clearance rules.[^s8]

## What does Lockheed Martin UK's apprenticeship involve?

## What does Boeing UK's apprenticeship involve?

Boeing UK apprenticeships are Level 3 in Aircraft Maintenance (Level 3 Engineering Technician standard), Aircraft Fabrication in Sheffield, Flight Services, Business Administration, and IT, HR and Talent Acquisition, lasting 18 months to three years.[^s11] Maintenance roles sit at military bases: AAC Middle Wallop, RAF Odiham, RAF Brize Norton, RAF Lossiemouth and Wattisham; accommodation is provided in year one of the maintenance scheme.[^s11]

No previous experience is expected.[^s11] Most programmes require security vetting, some roles carry nationality or place-of-birth restrictions, and no visa sponsorship is offered.[^s11] No salary, degree route or dates appear.[^s11]

## What does GKN Aerospace's apprenticeship involve?

GKN Aerospace's Aerospace Engineer Degree Apprenticeship is a four-year programme delivered with Weston College, mostly off-site in year one and on day release in years two and three, feeding into design, analysis, manufacturing engineering and quality roles at Filton and Western Approach.[^s12] Minimum entry is two A levels at A*-C including maths and physics or chemistry, or an equivalent engineering Level 3 diploma with further maths and mechanical principles units.[^s12]

The page shows a deadline of Friday 21 February with no year, and says applicants living outside the EU need a UK work permit before starting.[^s12] No salary is stated.[^s12]

## What does JLR's apprenticeship involve?

JLR runs a Level 3 Advanced Apprenticeship, three Level 6 degree apprenticeships (Applied Professional Engineering, Digital and Technology Solutions, Supply Chain and Procurement) and a Level 7 Finance apprenticeship, all four years long and all degree routes with the University of Warwick.[^s13] Pay is £26,092 on Level 6 and 7 and about £17,393 on Level 3.[^s13]

Applications opened in early February 2026 and closed in February or March; the 2026 round is closed.[^s13] There is no CV or cover letter and no experience is required.[^s13]

The Level 6 Applied Professional Engineering route leads to a BEng (Hons) with Warwick Manufacturing Group, based in the West Midlands and Halewood.[^s14] Entry: GCSE maths and English language at grade 5 plus three more GCSEs at 5 including a STEM subject (or a Level 3 engineering qualification), A level maths at B and a second A level at C in physics, chemistry, engineering or design and technology.[^s14]

## What does Nissan Sunderland's apprenticeship involve?

## What do the Royal Navy, Army and RAF apprenticeships involve?

The Royal Navy trains ratings on Intermediate Apprenticeships, usually taking 12 to 18 months after 10 weeks' basic training at HMS Raleigh, with Accelerated Apprenticeships in engineering; it says a rating can earn up to £48,000.[^s33]

The Army lists over 39 apprenticeships from Level 2 to Level 4, with Level 6 and 7 later in a career, taking one to four years on a full soldier's salary.[^s34]

The RAF offers 23 apprenticeship roles from Level 2 to Level 7, paying £25,200 from day one, over one to three years; ages are mostly 16 to 47 and nationality, residency and fitness criteria apply per role.[^s35]

## What does the Dyson Institute's degree apprenticeship involve?

The Dyson Institute awards its own BEng (Hons) Engineering degree apprenticeship over four years at Dyson's Malmesbury campus, with no tuition fees.[^s36] Entry is at least AAB at A level including A grades in maths and one other STEM subject, followed by tests, a phone interview and an assessment day.[^s37] Salary and dates are not published.[^s36]
