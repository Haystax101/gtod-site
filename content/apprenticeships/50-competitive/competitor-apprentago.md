## In one line

Apprentago is a free-to-students UK apprenticeship and work experience platform (AI CV builder, application tracker, job matching, Gemini-based AI assistant) run by DegreeApprenticeMedia LTD, which earns money from schools, employers and a separate paid Skool community rather than from young people.[^s1][^s5][^s7][^s17]

## Key facts

- Operating company: DegreeApprenticeMedia LTD, trading as Apprentago, company number 14847961, registered in England and Wales, address 50 Marston Road, Ilford, Essex, IG5 0LY; contact admin@apprentago.com.[^s5][^s6]
- Founder and CEO: Carlo Weaver, a former NatWest Markets degree apprentice; the Ilford Recorder reports the platform launched in November 2023.[^s2][^s12]
- Student pricing as at 3 September 2026: free, with no application cap and no paid student tier on any page opened.[^s3][^s4]
- Employer pricing as at 3 September 2026: free vacancy posting; an SME package charging £1,000 plus VAT as a success fee against a grant of up to £4,000; a Starter partnership at £6,000 a year; a Sector Partner package on custom pricing.[^s7]
- School pricing: a paid yearly subscription is required for tracking, dashboards and reporting, but the price is not published.[^s8]
- Separate Skool community: $20 a month, 40 members as at 3 September 2026.[^s17]
- User numbers claimed on Apprentago's own pages on the same day: "650,000+" (home, employer, schools), "600,000+" (CV builder, June 2026 blog), "350,000+" (application tracker, old employer page), "over 500,000 active users" (home) and "4,000 daily active student users" (mission pages).[^s1][^s3][^s4][^s7][^s8][^s14][^s20][^s29]
- Schools claim: "180+ schools/colleges" on the marketing pages, described in the June 2026 blog as 150 schools and 30 colleges paying subscribers.[^s7][^s8][^s20]
- AI: the assistant and CV tools run on Google Cloud and Gemini models, hosted in the London (europe-west2) region; the terms warn that the AI can make mistakes.[^s5][^s6]
- Vacancy sources: no page opened mentions the GOV.UK Find an apprenticeship API or any third-party feed; the terms describe employer-posted vacancies and student "self-added" placements, and the June 2026 blog mentions an NHS apprenticeships integration.[^s5][^s20]
- Refund policy, free trial terms and cancellation terms: not stated on any page opened.[^s5][^s7][^s8]
- Privacy policy effective and last updated 25 August 2026; Carlo Weaver is named as the data protection contact.[^s6]

## Who runs Apprentago and when was it founded?

The legal entity is DegreeApprenticeMedia LTD, company number 14847961, trading as Apprentago, with a registered address in Ilford, Essex.[^s5][^s6] The founder and CEO is Carlo Weaver, who the Ilford Recorder reports grew up in Hainault, was homeless with his mother in 2017, gained GCSEs at New City College and completed a software engineering degree apprenticeship at NatWest Markets on a £30,000 starting salary.[^s12]

The Ilford Recorder article (undated on the page as retrieved) says Weaver launched the platform in November 2023 and that a £100,000 investment offer was withdrawn at the last minute.[^s12] Apprentago's own mission pages do not give a founding date.[^s2][^s14] The Apprentago podcast's first episodes were published in September 2023, which predates the platform launch.[^s11]

The team listed on the mission page as at September 2026: Jonathan Zephir (tech adviser, described as a VP of Engineering at Citi), Ash Ali (strategic mentor and first shareholder, CEO of Uhubs), Richard Waddington (investor and business strategy mentor), Akshay Ruparellia (technology partner, CEO of Brdge AI) and Saachin Bhatt (AI strategy, co-founder of Brdge AI).[^s14] The June 2026 blog post says Waddington became Non-Executive Chair, Ruparellia became a technology equity partner, James Kimble joined as Head of Partnerships, and a seed round was due to open in July 2026 with no amount given.[^s20]

Weaver's mission page says he won "Small Business Influencer of the Year" at the British Business Awards in 2024; the home page also lists a BBC London interview, a Windsor Castle roundtable and a House of Lords invitation. None of these were checked against the awarding bodies.[^s1][^s14]

## What does the Apprentago student product include?

As at 3 September 2026 the student product, accessed at student.apprentago.co.uk, is a web app with Google sign-in and Apple sign-in, positioned for young people aged 14 and over.[^s1][^s6][^s15] The home page lists six components: an AI CV builder, an application tracker, job matching by preferences and dealbreakers, employer profiles with role videos, "Apprentago AI" (a Gemini-based assistant for apprenticeship questions and interview preparation) and a career dashboard with interview scheduling.[^s1]

Feature by feature, based only on Apprentago's own pages:

- Vacancy search: the FAQ says students browse apprenticeships by job role, industry or location and apply directly through the platform; the student portal describes "thousands" of apprenticeships with one-click apply.[^s3][^s15] The June 2026 blog adds work experience listings with a map view and postcode search, and an NHS apprenticeships integration.[^s20]
- Alerts: not mentioned on any page opened.[^s1][^s4]
- Application tracking: one dashboard for all applications, with notes and embedded social-media videos inside job descriptions.[^s4]
- CV tools: an AI builder claiming "10+" personalised examples, a five-step flow, multiple CV versions and ATS compatibility.[^s1][^s3]
- Interview and test practice: interview preparation through the AI assistant plus a free ten-lesson "Video Interviews A-Z Guide" course; no aptitude or situational judgement test practice is mentioned.[^s1][^s24]
- Employer lists: employer profiles with videos; the employer page names KPMG, Deutsche Bank, DHL and NatWest, and the old employer page shows logos including Bentley, Lloyds, BAE Systems, Bank of America and Amazon.[^s1][^s7][^s29]
- Community: a separate paid Skool community (see pricing below); the "coming soon" page links to a community waitlist.[^s17][^s27]
- Coaching: the AI assistant is described as a coach; no human coaching is offered on the pages opened.[^s6]
- Academy: three student resources, all shown without a price and appearing free: "Apprenticeships 101" (10 lessons, about 44 minutes, last updated 2 June 2026), the "Video Interviews A-Z Guide" (10 lessons) and an 80-page ebook.[^s9][^s22][^s23][^s24]
- Podcast: five episodes in season one on the Apprentago site, all from autumn 2023, with a second season promised for "late 2025" that had not appeared as at September 2026.[^s11][^s18]

The site talks about "the Apprentago App" on its account deletion page, and the June 2026 blog targets a mobile app launch for September 2026, but no App Store or Google Play link appears anywhere on the site.[^s20][^s26] The public site has no dedicated pricing, FAQ or how-it-works page: the pages sitemap lists 21 URLs and the students hub page only links out to the CV builder, tracker, Academy and blog.[^s19][^s21]

> [!GAP]
> No app store listing was found. GTOD should search the Apple App Store and Google Play for "Apprentago" after September 2026 to see if the promised mobile app shipped, and check whether it introduces in-app purchases.

## How much does Apprentago cost?

Student tier, as at 3 September 2026: free. The CV builder and application tracker pages both say the platform is "100% free for students" with no limit on the number of applications.[^s3][^s4] No paid student tier, premium CV feature, trial period or upsell appears on any page opened, and the terms of service contain no subscription, cancellation or refund clauses for students.[^s5]

Employer tiers, as at 3 September 2026:[^s7]

- Free platform: no setup fee, post apprenticeships and work experience, applicant tracking, employer profile. The June 2026 blog describes free posting as a permanent policy.[^s20]
- SME package: described as a 25% success fee model, worked as £1,000 plus VAT taken from a grant of up to £4,000, with grant eligibility support and training provider introductions. The June 2026 blog instead refers to helping SMEs access "£5,000" of apprenticeship grant funding.[^s20]
- Starter corporate partnership: £6,000 a year for one creator-led TikTok campaign, one careers-leader email campaign, one student email campaign and boosted visibility.
- Sector Partner: custom pricing for three TikTok campaigns, a co-branded lesson pack and boosted roles.

An older employer page still live on the site says the free tier covers up to five job postings and that listings go live within 24 hours; the current employer page does not repeat the five-posting limit.[^s7][^s29]

School tier: a paid yearly subscription unlocks student tracking, dashboards, Gatsby Benchmark and destination reporting; no price, trial or minimum term is published.[^s8] In the Cambridgeshire and Peterborough region, schools are funded through the Combined Authority under the Youth Guarantee rather than paying directly.[^s8][^s20]

Skool community: $20 a month, 40 members, three admins, offering a course, ebook, CV and LinkedIn workshops, monthly webinars and quarterly Q&A calls, with a stated intention to double the price after early members.[^s17]

Refund policy: not stated anywhere opened. Trial terms: not stated.[^s5][^s7][^s8]

## Where do Apprentago's vacancies come from?

Apprentago does not say on any page opened. The terms of service describe employers creating profiles and posting vacancies free of charge, and students self-adding placements sourced independently of Apprentago.[^s5] The June 2026 blog mentions an NHS apprenticeships integration searchable by postcode and work with three Jobcentre Plus sites in the East of England.[^s20] There is no mention of the GOV.UK Find an apprenticeship API, Adzuna, Indeed or any other feed.[^s3][^s4][^s5]

> [!GAP]
> Whether Apprentago's "thousands" of listings are employer-posted only, scraped, or pulled from the GOV.UK Find an apprenticeship API is unverified. A logged-in check of a sample of listings (looking for GOV.UK vacancy reference numbers or "apply on employer site" redirects) would settle it.

## Are Apprentago's user numbers and success stats substantiated?

No. All figures below come from Apprentago's own pages or its founder's interviews, and none is backed by a published methodology or third-party audit.

- Users: "650,000+" on the home, employer and schools pages; "600,000+" on the CV builder page and in the June 2026 blog; "350,000+" on the application tracker page and the old employer page; "over 500,000 active users" on the home page; "4,000 daily active student users" on both mission pages.[^s1][^s3][^s4][^s7][^s8][^s14][^s20][^s29] The Ilford Recorder reported "350,000 website hits", which suggests at least one of the user figures may be a page-visit count.[^s12]
- Outcomes: "900+ degree apprentice hires 2026" (employer and schools pages) and a claim that applicants "hear back 25% more" than on other platforms (home, CV builder and tracker pages), with no source for either.[^s1][^s3][^s7][^s8]
- Schools and CVs: "180+ schools/colleges", "200,000+ CVs built", "40,000+ experience hours tracked" (schools page); the June 2026 blog gives 150 schools and 30 colleges as paying subscribers and reports 71 of 80 Cambridgeshire and Peterborough schools logging in within two weeks.[^s8][^s20]
- Social reach: "35M+ careers-content views" (employer page); "over 14 million views" in the June 2025 LSi article; "ten million views" in the Ilford Recorder.[^s7][^s12][^s13]
- KPMG campaign: the employer page says "75K views in 24 hours" and "250+ neurodivergent applicants"; the June 2026 blog says "50,000 views in 1 day", "250 webinar sign ups" and five participants who later secured KPMG apprenticeships.[^s7][^s20]
- Giving: a commitment to reinvest 5% of top-line revenue in grassroots initiatives, also reported by LSi.[^s2][^s13]

**GTOD take:** the same-day spread between 350,000 and 650,000 users, and the drift from "hits" to "users" to "active users", means none of these figures should be repeated by Charge as fact. The 180 school figure is the most consistent number across pages and is the one most likely to be real, because it ties to paid contracts.

## What do Apprentago's terms and privacy policy say that matters?

- Minimum age: the terms set a minimum age of 12 with parental consent under 13 where required, while the privacy policy (25 August 2026) says registration does not currently enforce a minimum age.[^s5][^s6]
- Data hosting and AI: Google Cloud Platform in London; Gemini models for the AI coach; audio is converted to text and not retained; AI conversations are kept with the account and a retention schedule is under review.[^s6]
- Deletion: requests handled within 30 days; data already shared with employers or schools is not automatically deleted.[^s5][^s26]
- Analytics: Google Analytics on the WordPress marketing site.[^s6]
- Governing law: England and Wales.[^s5]

## Who does Apprentago sell to and partner with?

The B2B pitch targets schools and colleges, SMEs and corporates, training providers, and councils and combined authorities.[^s10] Named public-sector work as at June 2026: Youth Guarantee funding for a national work experience site, a Cambridgeshire and Peterborough Combined Authority contract covering every school and college in that region, three Jobcentre Plus sites, and discussions with other Youth Guarantee Trailblazers.[^s8][^s20] A June 2025 partnership with Events Apprenticeships made Apprentago that body's preferred search platform.[^s13]

**GTOD take:** the June 2026 blog states that Apprentago plans to scale nationally through Combined Authorities and integrate with Compass+, the careers tracking system used by schools. If that happens, Apprentago becomes the default tool inside school careers departments rather than a consumer app, which changes who GTOD competes with for a 16 year old's attention.

## GTOD take: strengths and weaknesses against Charge and the GTOD playbook

**GTOD take:** Strengths. Apprentago has a transactional product (CV, tracker, apply) that a school can mandate, a paid school base of around 180 institutions by its own count, public-sector funding, and a founder story that lands on TikTok and with the press. Its AI assistant is embedded in the same place a student applies, so it sees the student's CV and application history, which a standalone chatbot does not.

**GTOD take:** Weaknesses. The knowledge layer is thin: three courses, one ebook, one blog post in 2026, and a podcast that stopped in November 2023. The AI assistant is described as trained on Apprentago's own data, with a warning that it can make mistakes and no visible sourcing; that is exactly the gap Charge's sourced, dated knowledge base is meant to fill. Marketing numbers contradict each other across the site, which is a credibility risk if a school or journalist compares pages. The student community has 40 paying members, so there is no live peer community to match GTOD's.

**GTOD take:** Positioning. GTOD should not build a competing tracker or CV tool. Charge should be the accurate explainer (wages, funding rules, deadlines, standards) that a student uses alongside whichever application tool their school gives them, and GTOD's podcast and community should own the lived-experience layer Apprentago has let lapse. Watch for the September 2026 mobile app and any in-app paid tier, and re-check pricing pages quarterly.
