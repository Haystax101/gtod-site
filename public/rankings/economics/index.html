What's in each number

League table positions are each source's UK position for economics. QS and THE are world tables, filtered to UK institutions. Where a table only publishes bands, everyone in the band gets the band's middle position.

Satisfaction, graduate prospects, research and entry standards come from the Complete University Guide 2027 economics table (scores out of 100). International reputation averages QS's academic and employer reputation survey scores for economics.

Weighting. Every input is rescaled to 0–100 within this pool of 44 universities, then combined as a weighted average using the sliders.

Caveats worth knowing before you settle on anything

THE doesn't publish an economics-only table. Its closest is Business & Economics, which also counts business, management, accounting and finance, so universities with big business schools (Reading, Surrey, City) look stronger there than their economics departments alone would justify. Weight it down if that bothers you.

The Guardian's full 2027 economics table wasn't retrievable, so this uses the complete 2026 table. The Guardian is also where the biggest outliers come from: Heriot-Watt, Brighton, Essex and Stirling all sit in its top 10 but nowhere near it elsewhere.

QS positions beyond 250th in the world weren't retrieved; those departments count as unranked. QS and THE 2027 subject tables aren't out yet, so both are 2026 editions.
