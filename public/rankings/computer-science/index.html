What's in each number

League table positions are each source's UK position for computer science. QS and THE are world tables, filtered to UK institutions. Where a table only publishes bands, everyone in the band gets the band's middle position.

Satisfaction, graduate prospects, research and entry standards come from the Complete University Guide 2027 computer science table (scores out of 100). International reputation averages QS's academic and employer reputation survey scores for computer science.

Weighting. Every input is rescaled to 0–100 within this pool of 42 universities, then combined as a weighted average using the sliders.

Caveats worth knowing before you settle on anything

The Guardian's 2027 computer science table has only been retrievable down to 10th. Those ten use their 2027 positions. Everyone else uses their 2026 position (shown with ≈), floored at 11th, and anyone outside the 2026 top 25 whose position couldn't be found is scored as 30th (shown as 26+). Weight the Guardian down if that estimate bothers you.

THE's computer science table leans heavily on research environment and industry income, which is why St Andrews sits near the bottom of it despite topping student-facing tables. QS and THE 2027 subject tables aren't out yet, so both are 2026 editions.

QS positions beyond 300th in the world weren't retrieved; those departments count as unranked.
