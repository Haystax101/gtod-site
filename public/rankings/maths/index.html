What's in each number

League table positions are each source's UK position for maths. QS and THE are world tables, so they're filtered to UK institutions. Where a table only publishes bands (QS beyond 150, THE beyond 8th in the UK), everyone in the band gets the band's middle position.

Satisfaction, graduate prospects, research and entry standards come from the Complete University Guide 2027 maths table (scores out of 100). International reputation is the average of QS's academic and employer reputation survey scores for maths.

Weighting. Every input is rescaled to 0–100 within this pool of 44 universities, then combined as a weighted average using the sliders.

Caveats worth knowing before you settle on anything

THE doesn't publish a maths-only table. Its nearest is Physical Sciences, which mixes in physics, chemistry and earth sciences, so it undersells maths-heavy places like Bath and LSE. Weight it down if that bothers you.

The Guardian's full 2027 maths table wasn't retrievable, so this uses its 2026 maths table. Imperial is reported 2nd for maths in the 2027 edition (4th here). THE's 2027 subject tables and QS's 2027 subject tables aren't out yet; QS subject rankings normally land in spring.

QS's published list stops being visible for some UK departments beyond 300th in the world; they're treated as unranked.
