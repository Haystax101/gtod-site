What's in each number

League table positions are each source's UK position for law. QS and THE are world tables, filtered to UK institutions. Where a table only publishes bands, everyone in the band gets the band's middle position.

Satisfaction, graduate prospects, research and entry standards come from the Complete University Guide 2027 law table (scores out of 100). International reputation averages QS's academic and employer reputation survey scores for law.

Weighting. Every input is rescaled to 0–100 within this pool of 42 universities, then combined as a weighted average using the sliders.

Caveats worth knowing before you settle on anything

The Guardian's full 2027 law table wasn't retrievable, so this uses the complete 2026 table. LSE topped law in both editions. The 2026 Guardian table has several outliers that no other table agrees with, notably South Wales (8th), Edge Hill (20th) and Bangor (18th).

Scottish entry standards run high in the CUG measure (Glasgow scores 100, Strathclyde 90), because Scottish Highers convert to a higher UCAS tariff. Treat the entry column with care when comparing across the border, and remember Scottish law degrees train you in Scots law.

QS positions beyond 200th in the world weren't retrieved; those departments count as unranked. QS and THE 2027 subject tables aren't out yet, so both are 2026 editions.
